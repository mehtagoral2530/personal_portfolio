# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Goral Bhatt for healthcare market research, competitive intelligence, data analysis, clinical research support, and strategy roles. They need to understand her fit, see credible evidence of her work, and contact her quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Goral Bhatt's healthcare market-intelligence experience as a recruiter-facing portfolio that earns attention quickly and makes her résumé, relevant work, and contact details easy to use.

## Positioning

Goral combines biotechnology training with healthcare market research, competitive intelligence, tracker design, and disciplined research documentation across medical-device and biosimilar work.

## Operating Context

The page is typically scanned quickly on desktop or mobile by recruiters comparing candidates. Evidence comes from real résumé material: medical-device intelligence, recurring tracker maintenance, biosimilar research, regulated documentation, named employers, credentials, and tools.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- A responsive, single-page portfolio with anchored navigation, résumé download, email, phone, and LinkedIn contact routes.
- It must remain readable and fully usable at mobile and desktop breakpoints.
- The approved palette must remain unchanged.
- The user-approved healthcare-intelligence hero image and factual résumé material must be preserved.
- The eventual GitHub Pages build must be static-host compatible.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Goral Bhatt.
- Tone: precise, evidence-led, intelligent, and human; never generic recruiter copy or visual AI slop.
- Preserve the existing palette and use it with purpose rather than as interchangeable decoration.

## Evidence on Hand

- Résumé: `public/Goral-Bhatt-Resume.pdf`.
- Hero asset: `public/healthcare-intelligence-hero.jpg`.
- Experience and contributions: `app/page.tsx`.
- Portfolio copy must not invent clients, quantified outcomes, or proprietary research artifacts.

## Product Principles

1. Let credible healthcare-research work lead, rather than generic portfolio conventions.
2. Make the recruiter’s path obvious: understand the candidate, verify relevance, make contact.
3. Treat typography, spacing, and evidence structure as the primary design materials.
4. Build a memorable visual signature rooted in how healthcare intelligence is organized and read.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, readable type, clear link labels, and responsive layouts without text overlap.
