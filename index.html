TrialSignal

Medical studies, explained in plain English.

Clinical trial records are written for regulators, not for patients or curious readers. TrialSignal reads public records from ClinicalTrials.gov and tells you what was tested, what researchers found, and how sure we can be.

It checks the registry for updates while you browse, looks for the published paper in PubMed and Europe PMC, and only reports an FDA approval once a second source confirms it.

A learning tool. It does not decide trial eligibility or recommend treatments.
